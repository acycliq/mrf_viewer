Region accuracy

Each bar is one run, split by what the cells inside the region were called. Green is the class the region should have, red another excitatory class, then inhibitory, non-neuronal and Zero. The number on the right is green out of green plus red: of the cells called excitatory, the share called the right class. Strict: L4/5 IT in L5 counts as wrong, and L5 IT in L4/5 too.

The filter at the top changes every part of the page. A reads filter goes on every run. A corr filter needs the correlation MapMyCells gives each cell, so it goes on the MapMyCells runs only; the pciSeq runs then keep every cell and say "(every cell)". corr > 0 drops the cells MapMyCells had nothing to match on (no marker reads), which it still gives a class, mostly 004 L6 IT.

Class placement

Turned round: of all the cells a run called this class, anywhere in the picture, the share inside the class's own region. Cells near a region's edge, or in a layer that has no outline yet (stratum oriens, the white matter), count against it.

Heatmap

One row per region: how its cells split over the classes, as a share of the region's cells. The squares with a white edge are the classes the region should have. Pick a run on each side to compare them.
